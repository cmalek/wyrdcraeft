# Mission: Machine Assistance For Old English Work

## Why
I want to build dependable machine assistance for Old English study: OCR difficult sources, preserve philological evidence, turn outputs into structured data, and make that data usable by software and AI agents with minimal human intervention. I want practical workflows I can run, inspect, measure, and improve.

## Success looks like
- I can run a repeatable OCR-to-structured-data pipeline on difficult Old English sources and explain every stage.
- I can measure OCR quality in ways that matter for philological work, especially diacritics, special characters, layout, and provenance.
- I can prepare outputs that downstream software or agents can trust because they preserve evidence and uncertainty instead of hiding it.

## Constraints
- Prefer minimal-human-involvement workflows, but never at the cost of losing evidence or auditability.
- Teach through concrete repo workflows, artifacts, and evaluation gates.
- Keep raw witnesses, human corrections, and derived structured outputs separate.

## Out of scope
- General software engineering not tied to Old English tooling
- Language-learning lessons whose main outcome is better human reading or translation
- Fully opaque automation that cannot be inspected, measured, or corrected
